## Where phase 5 starts (2026-10-05, `feat/phase-5-prep` at e10e5f5)

- **Evaluation and registration (phase 3).**
  - `models.register` publishes a gated checkpoint as a `model` registry version with a model card.
  - `models.list|get` read them, and `registry.lineage` walks them.
  - The `deployable` artifact type is named in 03 but has no producer.
- **Decoding.**
  - `nemotron_transcribe@4` and `nemotron_live@1` share one streaming decoder (`cadence_nemo/pipeline.py`) with
    per-stream boosting.
  - Static boost lists (`boost.edit`, `cadence.boost_list/1`) and the primary profile `80ms`.
  - Manual tests (`transcriptions.new`, job kind `interactive`) against checkpoints, model versions and base models.
- **Queue.** Cards list `shadow` and `export` among their allowed job kinds, but nothing uses them yet. There is no
  `benchmark` kind, and a training step reserves the card's whole remaining cap.
- **Policy.** The preset rule `deployments` gates `deployments.promote|rollback`; nothing implements them.
- **Phase-4 pieces the flywheel builds on.**
  - Triage items (`tri_`, `triage.list|accept|correct|reject`) for disputed pseudo-labels.
  - Annotation batches with entity spans; mounts with `mount://` windows; `sdp_ingest` stereo split with per-channel
    VAD.
  - The playbooks `weekly-flywheel`, `improve-telephony` and `fix-names-terms` are listed, with their phase-5 steps
    skipped.
- **Spike A3** (`docs/spikes/A3-nemotron-finetune.md`, steps 4–5).
  - What worked: the prompt-aware ONNX export (`encoder_prompt.onnx` + `decoder_joint.onnx`, one per profile) and
    parity of 0.00 points, 199/200.
  - What did not: a Triton design (Python BLS, fp32 ORT on CUDA) that saturates at about 2.2 real-time streams.
  - Scripts are in `~/cadence-spikes/a3`, and the Triton 26.07 image is pulled on the stand.
- **The stand.** A 98 GB card with about 30 GB free beside vLLM and other services; `defaults.yaml` `compute`
  still describes the 48 GB card. No call recordings from Эра exist yet (phase-4 plan, decision 9).
- **Migrations.** The next free number is **0048** (0046 was never used; do not take it). Streams take the numbers in
  their row.

## Decisions taken for phase 5 (from the 2026-10-05 spec; decision log in `00`)

1. **Staging serving (R30).**
   - Triton 26.07 is the compose profile `serving`, with explicit model control and the `serving` volume.
   - The family's `serve` steps load and unload models.
   - `benchmark` is a new job kind that takes the card alone: it drains the card for up to 30 minutes and never
     preempts training.
   - `servingReserveGb` per card: on the stand the cap is 29 GB, the reserve 7 GB and training keeps 22 GB.
2. **Thresholds (R31)** as `deploy.*` defaults:
   - parity on the first 200 utterances by hash of the first target golden set, |ΔWER| ≤ 0.001 and ≥ 0.995 identical
     token sequences, through the staging server;
   - p95 time to final ≤ chunk + 100 ms at the target's concurrency.
3. **Signed promotions (R33).**
   - Records are JCS-canonical JSON, hashed with SHA-256 and signed with Ed25519, in one chain per delivery target.
   - The delivery bundle's `deliver.sh` refuses records not signed by the key pinned on the host and prints a
     `CADENCE-RECEIPT`; `promotions.verify` confirms it.
4. **Contract shapes.**
   - `models.export|parity|benchmark` are `POST /projects/{p}/models:<verb>`.
   - `deploymentTargets.*` are instance-wide; creating or editing one is an approval for everyone.
   - `deployments.new` creates a shadow deployment; `deployments.promote|rollback`; `promotions.list|get|verify`.
   - Deployments carry a target `slot`.
5. **Seams.**
   - The family roles `export`, `serve` and `parity reference` live in the NeMo pack; `parity_score@1`,
     `benchmark_score@1` and `shadow_score@1` are neutral core kinds.
   - Go and the web name neither Triton's stream protocol nor the family.
6. **GGUF and CPU targets** wait until a CPU deployment target exists; ROADMAP's "GGUF where a CPU target exists"
   has no target yet.

## Shared rules for every stream

- **Earlier rules.** The phase-2, 3 and 4 rules hold (`docs/review/2026-09-30-phase-2-plan.md` "Shared rules for
  every stream", `docs/review/2026-10-02-phase-3-plan.md` "Shared rules", `docs/review/2026-10-03-phase-4-plan.md`
  "Shared rules for every stream").
- **Contract markers.** Each stream adds contract paths under its own marker in `api/openapi.yaml`
  (`# ---- … (phase 5 · stream X) — add paths below this line`) and schemas under its marker at the end of
  `components/schemas`, then runs `make gen`. Verbs come only from 10 "Verb vocabulary"; phase 5 needs no new verb
  (`verify` now also covers promotions).
- **Migrations.** Each stream uses only its own migration number.
- **Seams.** Go and the web never name a framework, a family, Triton's stream protocol or a service
  (`internal/contract/seams_test.go`). The server kind `triton` and family names appear in targets as data; the
  stream client is the family pack's.
- **Production is out of reach.** No stream adds code that connects to a delivery target. A delivery target has no
  `endpoint`, and the bundle is the only path (non-negotiable 8).
- **The stand's card is shared.**
  - A stream's GPU tests stay ≤ 8 GB in its own container.
  - A stream may start its own Triton container for tests (≤ 7 GB, a private port) but never touches the stand's
    containers, its vLLM or the OASIS process.
  - Benchmarks taken beside other load are labelled as such and never quoted as results.
- **Signing.** No private key, pin or receipt from the stand is committed. Tests generate throwaway keys.
- **New step kinds.** Each gets `x-cadence` params with defaults in `defaults.yaml`, an entry in
  `worker/step-kinds.lock.json`, a help page and a conformance case where the runtime has one.

## Interfaces between streams

- **D1 → D2, D4.**
  - The `deployable` artifact (`cadence.deployable/1`; 03 "Artifacts") is what `serve` loads.
    `serving.modelDir` is the directory copied into the `serving` volume, and `serving.memoryMb` is the reservation.
  - `model_exports` rows are read through `models.get` `exports[]`.
- **D2 → D1.**
  - `nemotron_serve@1` params: `target` (a `dtg_` resolved to its endpoint in the lease), `concurrency`,
    `pace: realtime|fast`, `profile`.
  - Inputs `deployable` and `dataset`; outputs `hypotheses` (with `tokens`) and `serving_timings`.
  - D1's parity and benchmark pipelines wire it; until D2 merges, D1 tests against a stub kind with the same schema.
- **D2 → D3, D4.**
  - The `deployment_targets` table and the payload (02 "Deployment targets").
  - D3 subscribes to target creation for the genesis record through an in-process hook,
    `targets.OnCreated(func(ctx, tx, target))`.
- **D3 → D4.**
  - `promotions.Append(ctx, tx, kind, body) (Record, error)`, called in the transaction that decides the approval.
  - `delivery.Build(ctx, record) (artifactHash, error)` queues a control-plane job that writes the `delivery`
    artifact.
  - `promotions.verify` calls back `deployments.Confirm(ctx, tx, record)` to move the stage.
  - Records and receipts follow 02 "Promotion records" exactly.
- **D4 → F1, F2.**
  - Shadow `shadow_report` rows carry production-derived text under the captured-sample class (R28). F1's retention
    sweep takes them over; until then D4 evicts them after `deploy.shadow_artifact_retention_days`.
- **F1 → F2.**
  - The sample shape is fixed by the answer to R32 (а). Signals read only redacted text (R29); the judge refuses
    unredacted input.
