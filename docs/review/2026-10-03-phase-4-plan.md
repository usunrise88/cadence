## Decisions taken for phase 4 (owner-delegated; recorded in `07` "Open questions" by stream S)

1. **The first mount.**
   - **Mount and root.** Mount `corpora` has kind `local`, root `/cadence/corpora` on the staging host (the data LVM,
     116 GB free), and is read-only.
   - **Where it is visible.** Compose bind-mounts it into every worker service at `/mnt/corpora`. The control plane
     reads it only for indexing and previews, so it gets the same bind.
   - **Layout.** `/cadence/corpora/<source-name>/<revision>/…`, written once by the fetch scripts (stream B), never by
     Cadence.
   - **Writable mount.** A second mount, `exports` (kind `local`, `/cadence/exports`, read-write), is the target for
     exports and the backup mirror's mount option.
2. **The cache is the content store.**
   - **Pinning and eviction.** The CAS on the `artifacts` volume is the local NVMe tier. A shard is pinned while a
     queued or running job, or a promoted model, references its dataset version. Unpinned shards are evicted
     least-recently-used past `storage.cache_high_water_pct` (default 85) down to `storage.cache_low_water_pct` (70).
   - **Quota.** Per-project quotas count the bytes of the dataset versions a project froze
     (`storage.project_quota_gb`, default 200).
   - **What is never evicted.** Blobs an utterance or golden set needs and that live on no mount (imported audio) are
     never evicted. Only blobs with a `mount://` copy, or re-derivable shards, are.
3. **Index in place.**
   - **What an utterance keeps.** It keeps its `b3:` identity and gains `uris[]` (`mount://corpora/…#t=start,end&ch=N`
     for segments of a longer file).
   - **What ingest writes.** `sdp_ingest` streams the source files and writes only the segment manifest (no audio
     copy).
   - **Freezing.** `dataset_freeze` cuts and writes the 16 kHz mono segments into Shar shards in the CAS. That is the
     first copy. Segments are deterministic, so the cut is byte-identical on every host.
   - **Identity.** Before freeze, an utterance's hash is the BLAKE3 of the canonical 16 kHz PCM16 segment, computed
     while indexing. It therefore equals the hash of the WAV cut later, and leakage and fingerprints work before
     anything is copied.
4. **Draft dataset versions.**
   - `pipelines/data-ingest.yaml` ends in a draft (`dataset_version` with `frozen: false`, registry kind unchanged).
   - `datasets.preview` reports hours per language and split after filters; `datasets.freeze` (the verb `freeze`)
     runs the leakage check, the quality checks and the card, and then sets `frozen: true`.
   - Only frozen versions can be trained on, exported or adopted. Imports through `dataset_import` stay frozen at
     import (phase-2 behaviour).
5. **Auxiliary models (R26).**
   - **The kind.** A new registry kind `auxiliary` (`auxiliary/<name>`) holds LID classifiers, pseudo-label members
     and aligners.
   - **The payload.** Role (`lid | pseudolabel | align`), licence and `outputsCommercialUse: true`, languages, and
     either `{hfRepo, revision}` (a model a step loads) or `{service: {kind: grpc-asr, endpoint, protocol:
     oasis.v1}}` (a running service).
   - **Adoption.** It is approval-gated (registry scope, admin), and the adoption refuses
     `outputsCommercialUse: false`.
   - **Seams.** Go never names a framework or a service: the payload is data, and the worker pack that serves the
     role reads it.
6. **Pseudo-label ensemble (Granary-style, R26).**
   - **Shape.** One `pseudolabel_ensemble@1` core step (CPU) combines member hypotheses. Each member is its own step
     that produces `hypotheses` (the phase-3 artifact): `nemotron_transcribe@3`, `whisper_transcribe@1` (NeMo
     runtime, `transformers`, loaded per job) and `oasis_transcribe@1`.
   - **`oasis_transcribe@1`.** A new CPU pack `services` calls the auxiliary's endpoint and fails with
     `auxiliary-unavailable` when `GetModelInfo` does not answer.
   - **Agreement.** The text is kept when the pairwise WER after the scoring normalizer is ≤
     `pseudolabel.max_pairwise_wer` (0.15) for at least two members and LID agrees with the source's language.
     - The pick is the member with the lowest mean WER to the others, or OASIS's text when it is in the agreeing
       pair, since OASIS is itself a vote.
     - Disagreements get origin `pseudo-label:disputed` and go to the triage queue, never to training.
   - **Starting OASIS.** Cadence does not start OASIS. The pipeline's dryRun checks the service, and the playbook
     tells the person to run `scripts/serve.sh ensemble no-300m` on the host (8.6 GB, fits beside vLLM).
7. **LID.** The `lid_classify@1` step (NeMo runtime, speechbrain) uses `auxiliary/lid-voxlingua107`, and Whisper's own
   language detection is a second opinion from the whisper member's output. `mms-lid` is excluded (CC-BY-NC).
8. **Alignment (R51/R54).**
   - The `align_reference@1` step aligns a reference text to audio with a CTC model.
   - The model is `auxiliary/omniasr-ctc-1b` (the table below). MMS is excluded (CC-BY-NC).
   - With no allowed CTC model for a language, references stay unaligned. The emission-delay scorer then reports
     `n/a`, never a fake number.
9. **Synthetic calls stand in for real calls.**
   - **How one is built.** Stream B builds `calls-synth-sr` on the `corpora` mount: stereo 8 kHz G.711 μ-law WAVs, 2–6
     minutes each, in two channels.
     - Channel 0, the caller, is Serbian read or spontaneous speech from the public corpora below, with turns.
     - Channel 1, the bot, is speech from the host's local Qwen3-TTS service, with its text kept as the TTS script.
   - **What it exercises.** Stereo split, bot self-labelling, per-channel VAD, crosstalk detection, the noise bank
     from silences and the annotation workflow end to end.
   - **What it is not.** Source kind `synthetic`. The golden set frozen from it is named `golden-set/calls-synth-sr`
     and tagged `synthetic`. It is not the telephone golden set the ROADMAP means; that waits for real calls.
10. **Annotators.** A reviewer invited to a batch can play audio, but has no download link and no other data. This
    reuses the phase-1 reviewer role with a per-batch grant, and the media endpoints' signed links (R25).

## Shared rules for every stream

- **Earlier rules.** The phase-2 and phase-3 rules hold (`docs/review/2026-09-30-phase-2-plan.md` "Shared rules for
  every stream", `docs/review/2026-10-02-phase-3-plan.md` "Shared rules").
- **Contract markers.** Each stream adds contract paths under its own marker in `api/openapi.yaml`
  (`# ---- … (phase 4 · stream X) — add paths below this line`), and schemas under its marker at the end of
  `components/schemas`.
- **Migrations.** Each stream uses only its own migration number.
- **Seams.** Go and web code never name a framework, a family or a service (`internal/contract/seams_test.go`):
  `oasis`, `whisper` and `nemo` appear only in the worker, templates, auxiliary seeds and docs.
- **The stand's card is shared.**
  - Today 98 GB, with about 30 GB free beside vLLM and other services.
  - A stream's GPU tests stay ≤ 8 GB in its own container.
  - A stream never touches the stand's containers or the OASIS process.
- **New step kinds.** Each one gets its `x-cadence` params with defaults in `defaults.yaml`, an entry in
  `worker/step-kinds.lock.json`, a help page, and a conformance case where the runtime has one.

## Interfaces between streams

- **M → D.**
  - `mount://<mount-name>/<path>[#t=<start>,<end>][&ch=<n>]` is the one URI form, and `mounts.resolve(uri) → local
    path` lives in the worker harness (`cadence_worker.mounts`).
  - The worker reads mount roots from its lease (`lease.mounts: [{name, kind, root, readOnly}]`). M adds the field;
    D's steps use it.
- **D → X.**
  - The `segments` artifact (`cadence.segments/1`: `segments.jsonl` with `uri`, `hash`, `start`, `end`, `channel`,
    `role` (`caller | bot | mono`), `language?`, `text?`, `origin?`, `speaker?`, `vad`) is what the transcribe-style
    members read.
  - They read it as a dataset-like input: `materialize`d segments, or a `segments` → `dataset` cut on the fly by
    `dataset_freeze` in `dryRun` mode.
- **X → D.**
  - `pseudolabel_ensemble@1` outputs `segments` with `text`, `origin` (`pseudo-label`, `pseudo-label:disputed`),
    `confidence` and per-member texts in `hypotheses`.
  - `manifest_filter@1` drops disputed segments by default.
- **D/A → R.** Dataset version payload gains `frozen`, `quality` (the checks), `card` (Markdown hash), `stats` (R53
  series) and `shards[]` (hash, location, bytes, pinned). R's panels read only these.
- **A → G (phase 3).** `batches.freeze` calls the phase-3 `goldenSets.freeze` path with the batch's guidelines SHA and
  inter-annotator WER in the card.
