# internal/annotation
The annotation workflow (phase 4 · stream A; tables `annotation_batches` `anb_`, `annotation_items` `bit_`, `annotations` `ann_`, migration 0038; docs/spec/04-blocks.md "Annotation workflow", R27). `Prepare`/`Create` sample a batch from a frame — a segments artifact (`cadence.segments/1`, read with `ReadFrame`), named directly or through a dataset version's `segments` — over one role (the caller's channel by default), stratified by campaign, month, duration and confidence buckets (`Draw`: largest-remainder shares, at least one per stratum, seeded), pin `annotation/guidelines/<name>.md` at the project repository's head commit, and give each item its window (segment ± `annotation.context_s`, every channel; also its media id), the other party's turns, the prefill and the end-of-utterance gap. `Annotate` (one annotation per person and item, blind), `settle` (pending → agreed | disputed | excluded) and `Adjudicate` move items; `summarise` counts progress and the inter-annotator WER (`Words`/`Edits`: casefolded, punctuation stripped) over the first two transcripts of double items. `Freeze` (the approved `batches.freeze`) writes the accepted items as a segments artifact and a draft dataset version (through `data.Importer`), starts the cut (`dataset_freeze`, mode cut) and revokes the batch's reviewers; `OnDataset`, an output hook on `dataset` after the importer, freezes a golden-set batch's version as a golden set (`goldensets.Freeze`, its `annotation` card). `Resolve` resolves triage items (`triage.accept|correct|reject`) into human transcripts. Reviewer invitations are credentials (`internal/credentials/invitations.go`); audio windows are served by `internal/media`.
